HOW TO ADD PRODUCT PHOTOS
=========================

1. Save your product photos into THIS folder.

2. Use simple file names. Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only.
   Good:  red-football-boots.jpg
   Bad:   Red Football Boots (1).JPG

3. Then open this file:  src/lib/products.ts
   Find your product and set its imageUrl, for example:

   imageUrl: "/products/red-football-boots.jpg"

4. Save and the photo appears on the website.


PHOTO GUIDELINES
----------------

Format      JPG or WEBP is best. PNG also works.
Size        Around 1200 x 1200 pixels is ideal.
File size   Keep each photo under 300 KB if you can. Under 1 MB at most.
Background  A plain, light background looks most professional.
Lighting    Bright and even. Avoid heavy shadows.

Large photos make the website slow on mobile phones, which loses customers.
If a photo is more than 1 MB, reduce its size before adding it here. You can do
this free at squoosh.app in your browser.


TIP
---
It is fine to add products before you have photos. The website automatically
shows a clean coloured panel with the initials AS until you add a real photo.
